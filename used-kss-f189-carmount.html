Description

The Kinetic Support Systems (KSS) Vehicle Camera Mount F189 (VCM F189) is an ultra-versatile, compact and complete vehicle camera/equipment mounting system, capable of fixing a camera, or other piece of equipment, almost anywhere with respect to a vehicle. This is the Enhanced Kit originally offered by KSS.

Please study the manual thoroughly in order to fully take advantage of the Mount, and to use the Mount appropriately and safely.
