Description

This Arriflex 35-2C has been updated with a Cinema Products PL mount, large later generation eyepiece, Cinematography Electronics speed base and custom SD/NTSC video tap. The ground glass has TV/TV Safe markings and the eyepiece is a later-generation larger style. The package includes three later Arriflex 35-III 400 ft. magazines along with two early-generation 200 ft. magazines, one with a Sachtler & Wolf inventory plates.

The custom video tap was designed and manufactured by Stratton Camera and uses a single-chip Sony black-and-white NTSC camera coupled to the viewfinder through a beamsplitter. As a period SD video-assist system, image quality is limited compared with modern HD video taps and cameras.
